renversé
[rahn-vehr-SAY]

Upset, overturned. A step in which the body is tipped off balance. Usually performed with an en dehors or en dedans turn. The dancer begins in a small attitude, makes a demi-rond de jambe to the front, then tips the body back over the supporting leg while turning, creating a sweeping, off-balance sensation before recovering to the starting position.
